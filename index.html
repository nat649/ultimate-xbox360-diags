Reading the code

  1. Power the console on and wait for the red lights.
  2. Press and hold the Sync button on the front.
  3. Still holding Sync, press Eject once — the lights change to digit 1.
  4. Release Eject and press it again for each of the 4 digits.
  5. Every digit is 0–3, counted from the lit quadrants.
